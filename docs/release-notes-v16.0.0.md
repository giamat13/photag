# photag 16.0.0

## Features
- **Colour themes.** A new "Themes…" dialog (Preferences → Appearance) with 16 ready-made looks: Slate, Classic (the look before 15.0.0), Midnight, Graphite, Forest, Plum, Sunset, Ocean, Rose, two high-contrast themes, Light, Paper, Sepia, a colour-blind-friendly one and "Follow Windows". You can also make your own theme from a background and an accent colour, and load one from a file. The photo surfaces always stay neutral gray. The image viewer follows the chosen theme.
- **Many more editing tools in the Edit module:**
  - Whites, Blacks, Texture and Dehaze.
  - Vignette midpoint, feather, roundness and highlights.
  - Film grain with amount, size and roughness.
  - A **Tone Curve** (RGB, red, green and blue point curves you drag, plus parametric sliders).
  - A **Color Mixer** with hue, saturation and luminance for 8 colours, which becomes a **black & white mix** for black & white photos.
  - **Color Grading** for shadows, midtones and highlights, with blend and balance.
  - **Calibration**.
  - White-balance presets and an **eyedropper** that makes a clicked grey area neutral.
  - All of it is non-destructive, shows in History, and works with Reset and Previous Settings.
