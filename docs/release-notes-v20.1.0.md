# photag 20.1.0

## Small features
- **A loading screen**: photag now shows a loading screen while it starts, with the photag icon, a moving bar and what it is loading right now (your photos with their count, albums, people and keywords).
- **Right-click on a large picture**: the photo menu (copy, rate, flag, export...) now also opens when you right-click the picture in the Loupe, in Compare and in the slideshow, not only on the thumbnails in the grid.

## Fixes
- **Compare** compared with the wrong photo: after you picked another photo, the old "candidate" stayed and was compared with the new one. The candidate now follows what you select (the other selected photo, or the next one in the list).
- On a slow start the counts in the catalog (and **On This Day**) could stay at 0 until the next refresh; they are now drawn again when the photos have arrived.
