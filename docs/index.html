Your photos. On your computer. Nowhere else.

photag is a free photo and video manager — library, collections, face recognition, search by meaning, non-destructive editing and automatic backups. It works offline and never uploads your pictures.

Everything for a photo library that is really yours

Import from anywhere, find any picture in seconds, and keep it safe — without handing it to a service.

🗂️

A real photo library

Grid, loupe, compare and survey views, ratings, flags, colour labels, keywords, collections and smart collections.

🧑‍🤝‍🧑

Faces and people

Face recognition that runs on your PC groups the same person across years. Name them once.

🔎

Search by meaning

Type “beach at sunset” and find it. A local AI model — nothing is sent anywhere.

🛡️

Automatic backup

A verified daily backup of the catalog, settings and photos, with a one-click restore that can itself be undone.

📥

Import everything

Folders and memory cards, RAW files, Lightroom catalogs, digiKam, and Google Takeout straight from the ZIP files.

🎛️

Non-destructive editing

Exposure, tone, colour, crop and an Auto button. The original is always kept and can be restored.

Duplicates and cleanup

Find copies and similar shots, pick the best of a series, and clear screenshots and blurry frames in bulk.

Video, too

A full player and HandBrake compression that verifies the result before replacing anything.

🗺️

Map, timeline, On This Day

See where a photo was taken, jump to any month, and get a quiet reminder of what you shot years ago.

Open any picture — without adding it to anything

Right-click a picture → Open with → photag. It opens instantly in a clean viewer and your library stays exactly as it was.

  • Zoom, pan, rotate, full screen and next / previous picture in the folder
  • JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP, BMP, TIFF, HEIC, AVIF and RAW
  • An optional “Add to library” button — only when you want it
  • Make it your default and replace the Windows Photos app

Windows says the installer is blocked (Smart App Control)?

photag is not code-signed yet, and Smart App Control blocks every unsigned program. Use the install script below: Windows asks “Run / Cancel” instead of blocking, and nothing it unpacks is marked as downloaded from the internet. Or turn Smart App Control off in Windows Security.

Download windows-photag-install.bat

If the .bat is blocked too, paste this into PowerShell:

[Net.ServicePointManager]::SecurityProtocol='Tls12'; $w=New-Object Net.WebClient; $w.Encoding=[Text.Encoding]::UTF8; & ([scriptblock]::Create($w.DownloadString('https://github.com/giamat13/photag/releases/latest/download/windows-photag-install.ps1').TrimStart([char]0xFEFF)))
macOS and Linux notes

The macOS build is not notarized, so the first time use right-click → Open. On Linux you need a desktop with GTK or Qt WebKit; without one photag opens in your browser. Details: docs/LINUX_MACOS.md.

Private by design

photag runs a small server that listens only on your own computer. Your pictures, faces and catalog never leave it. The only things that touch the internet are ones you choose: checking for updates, optional AI tagging with your own key (a small thumbnail only), and map tiles.
