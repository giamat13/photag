# photag 17.0.0

## Features
- **A much bigger Edit module** (everything non-destructive, in all 16 languages, also in History):
  - **Detail:** sharpening with radius, detail and masking; luminance and colour noise reduction.
  - **Lens corrections:** distortion, lens vignetting, automatic and manual chromatic aberration, defringe.
  - **Geometry:** vertical and horizontal perspective, aspect, scale and offset, and **Upright** (Auto, Level, Vertical, Full) that finds straight lines and straightens the photo. Crop with fixed aspect ratios (1:1, 4:5, 5:7, 2:3, 3:2, 4:3, 16:9 …) and guides (rule of thirds, grid, golden ratio).
  - **Local adjustments (masks):** brush, linear and radial gradients, luminance range, colour range, sky, subject and background; add, subtract and intersect components, invert, 15 adjustments per mask and an amount. The selected mask is shown in red on the photo.
  - **Spot removal** (heal or clone, the source is found for you or dragged by hand) and **red eye**.
- **Presets, looks and settings:** an Amount slider for presets (0–200 %), more built-in presets, your own presets (save, import and export as files), **looks** from `.cube` colour tables with an amount, **snapshots** of the settings of a photo, and **Copy / Paste / Synchronize settings** between photos with the groups you choose (Ctrl+Shift+C / V / S).
- **Before and after:** side by side (Y) and split with a draggable line (Shift+Y); **clipping warnings** (J) show blown highlights in red and crushed shadows in blue.
- **Photo merge:** merge bracketed photos to **HDR** (exposure fusion, lined up automatically) and overlapping photos to a **panorama**; the result is added to the library (Photo menu).
- **The viewer's editor** has the simple new tools too: whites, blacks, clarity, texture, dehaze, noise reduction and grain.
