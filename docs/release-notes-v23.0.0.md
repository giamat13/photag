# photag 23.0.0

## Features

- **Series**: similar photos taken within 2 minutes of each other (the same thing photographed several times) are recognised automatically (after the photos were analysed, e.g. with Score All). Each of them shows a badge with the number of photos in the series; a click opens the whole series in Compare. The photos of a series share their keywords: a keyword added to or removed from one of them is added to or removed from the others (#42, #44).
- **Ask the AI which photo is best**: Compare and Rank have an "Ask AI" button. The AI (the provider, key and model of AI tagging) chooses the best photo and says why; its choice is marked and becomes the photo "Pick this, reject the others" keeps. In the AI tagging settings you choose what Rank does about it: ask every time (the default once the AI is set up), always use the AI, or never (the default while the AI is not set up) (#45).
