# photag 23.1.0

## Small features

- **New photos get their quality score automatically**: a few at a time in the background, while nothing else runs and not on battery. It is the program's own measurement (sharpness, exposure, contrast), no AI. Library > "Score New Photos Automatically" switches it off and on (#48).
- **Score All scores everything again**, also when every photo already has a score: press it after an update that improved the measurement, or when you want fresh numbers (#48).
